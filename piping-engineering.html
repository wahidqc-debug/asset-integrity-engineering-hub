Piping Engineering Suite

Educational pressure-design screening based on ASME B31.3. Verify the governing edition, fitting geometry, material properties and applicability independently.

Straight pipe and smooth bends

Paragraphs 304.1.2 and 304.2.1: pressure-only thickness. Bend radius is centerline radius; D is outside diameter. Not a substitute for listed fitting qualification, miter bend rules, external pressure or piping stress analysis.

t = PD / [2(SEW/I + PY)]. I = 1 for straight pipe and sidewall; intrados I = (4R/D − 1)/(4R/D − 2); extrados I = (4R/D + 1)/(4R/D + 2). Nominal selection screening = (t + c)/(1 − mill tolerance). Bend forming thinning must be addressed separately. Use independently verified E, W and Y.

Flow velocity and friction pressure drop

Single-phase incompressible flow, constant diameter, Darcy friction factor supplied by engineer. Excludes elevation and fittings unless entered as total K.

Hydrostatic test reference

B31.3 paragraph 345.4.2 screening: 1.5 × design pressure × applicable test/design allowable stress ratio where required. Enter ratio 1 only when appropriate. Stress limits, static head and component ratings must be checked separately.

Additional engineering checks

Branch reinforcement (304.3), pipe schedule tables (B36.10M/B36.19M), combined longitudinal stress and flexibility analysis, external pressure and listed fitting ratings are not implemented. Use approved design data. For multi-point inspection history use Thickness Intelligence.

Pressure-only capacity is not system MAWP. Remaining life is time to pressure-only thickness, not approved retirement life. No confidential data.
