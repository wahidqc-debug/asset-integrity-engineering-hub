Illustrative risk matrix

Score = ordinal probability category × ordinal consequence category. Bands: 1–4 Low, 5–9 Medium, 10–16 High, 17–25 Very high. These are illustrative choices, not API thresholds.

Do not use this score to establish inspection intervals, acceptance, deferral or operational decisions. Quantitative PoF, CoF, damage factors and inspection effectiveness calculations are not implemented. Consult API 580 and API 581 and approved procedures.
