Engineering use limitations

AIEH is a public beta learning and preliminary screening resource powered by SIGMA Oilfield Equipment Trading. Calculations and reference data may contain errors or omissions and have not undergone independent engineering validation. Verify inputs, equations, units, material data and applicable code editions independently. Do not rely on results alone for fitness-for-service, operating limits, inspection deferral or other safety-critical decisions. No certification or approval by standards organizations is implied.

Privacy and feedback

No account is required for the tools. Do not enter confidential asset information or personal data. GitHub Pages and external image providers may receive ordinary web request information. Do not include confidential information in public feedback.
