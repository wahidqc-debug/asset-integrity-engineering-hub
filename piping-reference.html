Material specification guide

Common examples: ASTM A106 Gr B (seamless carbon steel pipe), ASTM A53 Gr B (carbon steel pipe), API 5L (line pipe), ASTM A312 TP304L / TP316L (austenitic stainless pipe), ASTM A234 WPB (carbon steel wrought fittings), ASTM A105 (carbon steel forgings). These are identification aids, not material selection or allowable stress data.

Stainless schedules, flange pressure-temperature ratings, fitting dimensions and branch reinforcement remain outside this release. Do not infer flange rating from class number alone.
